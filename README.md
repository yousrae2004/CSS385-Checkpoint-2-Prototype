# Checkpoint 2: Prototype Proposal

## Elevator Pitch
Have you ever wanted to explore the world as a curious cat, venturing into a brand-new scenery?
A Cat's Quest is a cozy top-down game where you can adventure through town and find your way back home.

# Game Synopsis
In this prototype model, you play as a cat adventuring outside your home in a new town for the first time.
After finding yourself lost in this neighborhood, you can navigate your environment to explore outside your 
house and find your way back.

**Objective** The prototype objective is simple, allowing for the display of player mechanics and how they would look
before storyline implementation. You can explore your home and start the backyard scene, then move back into your
house to 'win'.

**Mechanics** Players can control their character around freely using the arrow keys or WASD, exploring each scene
to find their way around.
